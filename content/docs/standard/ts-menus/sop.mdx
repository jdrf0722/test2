## Introduction [#introduction]

The RFF Menu is the central module for inventory management and control within the application, organized into four major functional areas that range from item maintenance to the management of warehouse slots (locations).

## RFF Menu [#menu]

### Inquiry Option[#master]

- Option: **I1**  
  Product Inq: Inventory Commitment Inquiry. This option/panel shows Item information, the warehouse where it is located, the avail of the item across multiple dates. You need to select a prodcut category.

- Option: **I2**  
  Customer Account Profile: New Account Profile program. You can enter a customer code and maintain it's new account profile. You can modify here it's email, the account type, the purchase and buying desicion, contract information, if the company is currently doing bussiness with Sysco, the sysco house, suppliers information and buckhead beef items information.

- Option: **I3**  
  Open A/R Invoices: Customer Open A/R Invoice Inquiry. In this panel/option you can enter a customer code and the open invoices information for the customer you have entered will be displayed.

- Option: **I4**  
  Box Inquiry: Box File (Production) Inquiry. Here you can enter a box number and see the order information related to that box, date/time and item information.

- Option: **I5**  
  Customer Sales History: Customer Master File Maintenance. Here you can enter a customer code and see it's related information, the customer name, code, address, credit contact, terms code, etc… You can see too the Monthly and Weekly Figures.

- Option: **I6**  
  Golden Rod Printing: Golden Rod Invoice Report. This option/program will print a report of the golden rod invoice information to a printer. You can maintain here the golden rod invoice information. You can delete and modify it's data.

- Option: **I7**  
  A/R Invoice History: Customer Open A/R Invoice Inquiry History. In this panel/option you can enter a customer code and the history of invoices information for the customer you have entered will be displayed. All the information about invoices the customer has had, will be displayed.

- Option: **IH**  
  Inv History rpt: Sales Rep Invoice Detail Report. In this option/panel you can select a date range and display the sales rep Invoice information on screen, you can export it to excel. The panel shows a summary of Lbs, sales and sales cost by the date range you selected. The sales report includes customer, invoice, item, description, lbs, unit price, sales cost, ext sell, ext cost, gp$, gp%, cust name, inv date, etc.

- Option: **SA**  
  Sales Analysis: Sales Rep OpenSales Order Analysis. In this panel/option you ca select a load date and display the sales rep open invoices for the load date you have selected. You can filter by route, by sales rep and by operator, you can export a report to excel and pdf. The panel shows a summary at the end of order, sales$, lbs, gp$ and gp%. You can display a open sales order analysis by zone and by customer in this panel.

- Option: **SM**  
  Sales Mgt Menu: This option will lead you to Menu SMG (Sales Managment Menu).

- Option: **A1**  
  Open Ord Inq: Sales Rep OpenSales Order Analysis. In this panel/option you ca select a load date and display the sales rep open invoices for the load date you have selected. You can filter by route, by sales rep and by operator, you can export a report to excel and pdf. The panel shows a summary at the end of order, sales$, lbs, gp$ and gp%. You can display a open sales order analysis by zone and by customer in this panel.

- Option: **CM**  
  Cmm Display: Invoice Commision Analysis. In this panel/option you can select a date range and display a sales rep invoice commision analysis. You need acces to diferent salesrep so you can display their data. This panel display the sls rep, the customer, number of orders for the span of time you have selected, ext sell, etc.

- Option: **SP**  
  A/R Stmt Prt: Customer Statement Profile. In this option/program you can maintain cust statement profiles, you can enter one, delete them, create a new one and modify them. You can modify the statement aging date, min A/R Balance, print negative invoices?, print zero invoices?, lines and customer of a customer statement profile.

- Option: **SI**  
  Slot Inquiry: Inventory Item Slot Inquiry. In this option/program you can enter an item code, select a warehouse, receipt date, brand, weight over and age over, then the data of the inventory item slot you have selected will be displayed. Here you can see the Bin slot, number of units, total weight.

- Option: **R0**  
  EDI: This option will lead you to Menu EDI (EDI Processing).

- Option: **R1**  
  StkRm Rpt: This option/program will print a pdf report to the selected printer about slot location report (including size) by category/product group, you can sort by a set of parameters. The pdf will display slot information.

- Option: **R2**  
  OpnOdr Rpt: Open Orders Reports. In this panel/option you can enter a order date cutoff, a range of customer code, a range of order statuses, a range of routes and you can display the data to the screen or a printer. The program will generate an Open Orders report based on the paramters you have selected. The report will display the order numbers, delivery dates, customer codes, ship-to-names, sales person man and the route/stop.

- Option: **1**  
  Order Entry: Order Entry Heading. This program/option will let you create and maintain order headers. You can create, delete and modify orders headers here in this program. This version of order entry does not counts with a panel(GUI).

- Option: **3**  
  Order Status: Open Order by Operator Inquiry. In this panel/option you will be able to see the order under your name. You can see here the statuses, entry , delivery date, and the route of your orders.

- Option: **4**  
  Order Locator: This is the order locator. Order Status Inquiry. You can search here information realted to orders. This option/program will let you visualize the information there is for an order, you can search an order here by remote order number, by the customer the order is attached, by it's order number, by phone number, by who was it ordered by and by the customer is being bill to. You could search it by PO number too.

- Option: **5**  
  Order Review by Operator: Non-Invoiced Order Tracking. Here you can list the non invoiced orders by operator and see it's information.

- Option: **CH**  
  Order Change Log Report: Change Log Report. In this panel/option you can create a report about every change that has been made to an order and send it to a printer. This report will show the order number, it's information, the change and the operator who made the change.

- Option: **PE**  
  Pickup Entry: Pickup Request panel. In this option/panel you can maintain pickup requests. Here you can create, delete and modify pickup requests, you can see its information and look up for existing ones.

- Option: **1G**  
  Order Entry (GUI): Sales order entry. This is the GUI version of option 1. Here you can maintain sales orders, you can create new ones, delete them, modidy them and look up for existing ones. You can add lines here, you can print the order, you can acces pickup entry from here and you can do order guide.

- Option: **1P**  
  Pick Ticket Printing: Pick Ticket Printing. In this option/panel you can select the pickups that are ready to print and print them. You can select a printer. You can print multiple to print or just one..

- Option: **4G**  
  Order Locator (GUI): Order/Invoice Lookup. In this panel/option you can look up for a customer and see it's credit information. Here you can look up for orders/invoices too.

  ### Maintenance/Other Options[#maintenance]

- Option: **M3**  
  Customer Buyer/Email: Customer Emails. In this panel/option you can look for a customer, see it's buyer name, phone, fax and the emails the customer has attached, you can add new emails, delete them and modify them. This option works as a customer emails maintenance.

- Option: **M5**  
  Sales Pricing Tool: In this option/program you will able to use the Sales Pricing Tool.

- Option: **M6**  
  Statement Printing : Customer Statement Profile. In this option/program you can maintain cust statement profiles, you can enter one, delete them, create a new one and modify them. You can modify the statement aging date, min A/R Balance, print negative invoices, lines and customer of a customer statement profile.

- Option: **M7**  
  Inv. Message: Message Processing. Here you can select a company wide or a customer to process a message that they will get, or you can create a report to excel with the information of the messages that have been processed .

- Option: **P1**  
  Price Lt. - Deals - Price Bk: This option will lead you to Menu SL2 (Sales price book / price letters / deals).

- Option: **SB**  
  Sales Analysis Menu: This option will lead you to Menu SAM (Sales Analysis).
